Free and low-cost legal help for storage renters

If your storage unit is headed to a lien sale or auction, or you think the facility broke the rules, these free legal aid and pro bono services can help. Most have income limits.

Every link checked Oct 27, 2026.

Not paid partners. These are nonprofit legal aid, court and bar association services. We earn nothing from them and they don't pay us.

Anywhere in the U.S.

  • LawHelp.org: Find free legal aid, self-help information and free legal forms from nonprofit legal aid providers in your state.
  • Legal Services Corporation: find legal aid near you: Enter your address to find the LSC-funded civil legal aid organization that serves your area.
  • ABA Free Legal Answers: If you qualify, ask volunteer lawyers a civil legal question online and get an answer by email. Available in participating states only.

ABA Free Legal Answers was not open in Ohio when we last checked its participating-state list. Use the state links below instead.

By state

Indiana

  • Indiana Legal Help: Statewide portal: find legal services by ZIP or county, free legal clinics, and Indiana court forms.
  • Indiana Legal Services: Nonprofit law firm giving free civil legal help to eligible low-income Hoosiers in all 92 counties. Call 317-631-9410.

Ohio

  • Ohio Legal Help: Free legal information, how-to guides, court forms and connections to organizations that give legal advice and representation.

What to have ready when you call

  • Your storage rental agreement (lease), including any addendum.
  • Every notice the facility sent you, and the envelope or email it came in (the date matters).
  • Your payment history and the amount the facility says you owe.
  • The sale date, time and place or website, if the notice lists one.

This page is general information, not legal advice. Legal aid offices decide who they can help.
