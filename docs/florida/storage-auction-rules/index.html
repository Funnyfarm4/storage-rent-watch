Florida Storage Unit Auction Rules (Fla. Stat. 83.801-83.809): Timeline, Notices and Your Rights

How a Florida storage unit gets sold under the Self-storage Facility Act: the 5-day lockout, 14-day notice, two weeks of ads, redemption and leftover money.

Quick answer

  • If rent isn't paid, the facility can lock you out after 5 days from the due date, without notice The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[5].
  • To sell, it must send a written notice demanding payment within at least 14 days after delivery, and post it at the facility or on the unit The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6].
  • After that deadline, the sale must be advertised once a week for 2 consecutive weeks in a local newspaper, and the sale can't happen until at least 15 days after the first ad The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6].
  • You can stop the sale any time before it happens by paying the lien amount plus the facility's reasonable expenses The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6].
  • A late fee of $20 or 20% of monthly rent, whichever is greater, is treated as reasonable, and the fee must be stated in your agreement The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[7].
  • Leftover sale money is held for you, but you must claim it within 2 years of the sale The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6].

Florida's self-storage lien law is the "Self-storage Facility Act," sections 83.801 through 83.809 of the Florida Statutes The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[2] The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[1]. In it, the renter is called the tenant and the facility is the owner (which includes an operator, manager or agent) The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[3]. Below is a plain-English summary of the statute text. Your rental agreement can add rights and duties on top of the act The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[8], so read it too.

The facility's lien

The facility has a lien on all personal property in your unit, whether or not you own it, for rent, labor and other charges, plus expenses for preserving the property and reasonable costs of selling it. The lien attaches the day your property is brought to the facility. If someone holds a perfected security interest naming you as the debtor (for example, a lender on financed property), the facility must notify them when you default The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[4].

The timeline, step by step

Step 1: Lockout after 5 days

If you don't pay rent when it's due, the facility may, without notice, deny you access to your property once 5 days have passed since the due date. It can do this without going to court if it can be done without a breach of the peace The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[5]. A lockout is not a sale: you still have time to pay.

Step 2: Written notice of the lien

To sell, the facility must give you written notice. It must be delivered in person, by email, or by first-class mail with a certificate of mailing to your last known address, and conspicuously posted at the facility or on the unit The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6]. Your last known address is the one in your latest rental agreement or in a later written change-of-address notice you gave by hand delivery, first-class mail or email The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[3]. Mailed notice is presumed delivered when it's deposited with the Postal Service, properly addressed and stamped The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6].

The notice must include The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6]:

  • an itemized statement of the claim, showing the amount due and the date it became due
  • a description of your property matching (or reasonably similar to) the one in your rental agreement
  • a demand for payment within a stated time of at least 14 days after delivery of the notice
  • a conspicuous statement that, unless you pay in time, your property will be advertised and sold or otherwise disposed of at a specified time and place
  • the owner's name, street address and phone number so you can respond

If the facility emails a notice of a pending sale and gets no response, return receipt or delivery confirmation from your email address, it must also send the notice by first-class mail with a certificate of mailing before proceeding with the sale The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6]. Keep your mailing and email addresses current with the facility, in writing.

Step 3: Advertising the sale

After the deadline in the notice passes, the sale must be advertised once a week for 2 consecutive weeks in a newspaper of general circulation in the area where the facility is. If there's no such newspaper, the ad must instead be posted in at least three conspicuous places in the neighborhood at least 10 days before the sale The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6]. The ad must include The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6]:

  • a brief, general description of what's believed to be in the unit
  • the facility's address and your name
  • the time, place and manner of the sale

Step 4: The sale

The sale must take place at least 15 days after the first ad. It can be held on a public website that customarily runs personal property auctions, it must follow the terms in the notice, and it must be conducted in a commercially reasonable manner The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6]. A buyer in good faith takes the property free of your claims even if the facility didn't follow the rules The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6], so act before the sale.

Vehicles and boats: if rent and other charges tied to a motor vehicle or watercraft stay unpaid for 60 days, the facility may sell it under these rules or have it towed. Once a wrecker takes it, the facility isn't liable for it, and the towing company must follow Florida's separate towing notice and sale law, section 713.78 The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6].

How to stop the sale

Before any sale or other disposition, you can redeem your property by paying the amount needed to satisfy the lien plus the reasonable expenses the facility incurred enforcing it. Once you pay, the facility must return your property The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6]. Ask for an itemized payoff amount in writing, pay in a way that leaves a record, and get a receipt and written confirmation that the sale is cancelled. If the sale date is close and you can't pay, contact free legal help right away.

If you don't redeem or pay the lien, the law treats you as having abandoned the unit, and the facility can take the space back The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6].

Leftover money from the sale

The facility pays its lien from the sale money. Anything left over is held by the facility for delivery to you on demand, and it must send you a notice of the balance in person or by first-class mail with a certificate of mailing. If you don't claim it within 2 years after the sale, the money is treated as abandoned and the facility has no further obligation to pay it The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6]. Ask the facility for it directly and in writing, and keep a copy.

If a secured lender's lien comes ahead of the facility's lien, the sale money is held for that lender first The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6].

Other Florida rules to know

  • Late fees: the facility may charge a reasonable late fee for each period rent is unpaid, but the amount and conditions must be stated in your rental agreement or an addendum. A fee of $20 or 20% of monthly rent, whichever is greater, is considered reasonable. It may also charge reasonable fees for rent collection or lien enforcement expenses The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[7].
  • Value limits: if your agreement limits the value of what you can store, that limit is treated as the maximum value of your property The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6]. Read that clause before you store anything valuable.
  • Military service: your rental agreement or application must ask whether you're a member of the uniformed services The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[7]. If you are, tell the facility and ask legal help about your federal protections.
  • No living in the unit: no storage space may be used for residential purposes The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[3].
  • Rent increases: the act has no section setting a rent-increase notice period, so your rental agreement controls The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[1]. See My storage rent went up. What can I do?

Frequently asked questions

How long before a storage unit is auctioned in Florida?
The facility can deny access 5 days after rent is due. To sell, it must give written notice with at least 14 days to pay, then advertise once a week for two consecutive weeks, and the sale must be at least 15 days after the first ad The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[5] The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6].
Can a Florida storage facility send the auction notice by email?
Yes, notice can be delivered in person, by email, or by first-class mail with a certificate of mailing to your last known address, and it must also be posted at the facility or on the unit. If the facility emails a notice of a pending sale and gets no response, return receipt or delivery confirmation from that address, it must also mail the notice by first-class mail with a certificate of mailing before going ahead The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6].
Can I stop a storage auction in Florida?
Yes. Before any sale or other disposition, you can pay the amount needed to satisfy the lien plus the reasonable expenses of enforcing it. The facility must then return your property The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6].
What is the maximum storage late fee in Florida?
Florida allows a reasonable late fee for each period rent is unpaid, stated in the agreement or an addendum. The law says a late fee of $20 or 20% of monthly rent, whichever is greater, is reasonable and is not a penalty. The facility may also charge reasonable fees for collection or lien-enforcement expenses The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[7].
Do I get the extra money if my Florida storage unit sold for more than I owed?
The facility keeps the balance for delivery to you on demand and must notify you of it. If you don't claim it within 2 years after the sale, it's treated as abandoned and the facility no longer owes it to you The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[6].
Can I live in my storage unit in Florida?
No. Florida's definition of a self-service storage facility says no individual storage space may be used for residential purposes The Florida Legislature, Online Sunshine (2026 Florida Statutes)[3].
