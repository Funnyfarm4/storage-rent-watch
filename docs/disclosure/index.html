Disclosure & Privacy

Paid partner links

Storage Rent Watch is reader-supported. Some pages include links to storage marketplaces, currently SpareFoot, Storage.com and Neighbor. These links are labeled "Paid partner link." If you click one and rent storage, we may earn a commission or referral fee at no extra cost to you. Until a partner program approves us, these links go to the partner's ordinary public website and earn nothing.

  • Partners do not review, edit or approve our guides, and they cannot pay to change the rules we report.
  • State rules on this site come only from statute text and official government pages, linked on each page.
  • We only show the partner box where comparing storage options might genuinely help, for example after a rent increase or when you have to move your things.
  • We don't claim partners are cheaper than anyone else. Compare quotes yourself.

Free legal help links are not paid

Our "Free legal help" boxes and Free legal help page link to nonprofit legal aid, court self-help and bar association pro bono services. They are free or low-cost, they don't pay us, and we earn nothing from them.

This disclosure follows the U.S. Federal Trade Commission's guidance on endorsements and affiliate links.

Privacy

This site runs no tracking scripts, ads or analytics, and it sets no cookies. We don't collect your name, email or any other personal information. When you click a partner or legal aid link, you leave our site, and that site's own privacy policy applies. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under GitHub's privacy statement.
