About Storage Rent Watch

Storage Rent Watch answers everyday questions storage renters search for: my storage rent went up, what can I do?, how long before my unit is auctioned?, and how do I stop the sale? The answers are buried in state statutes. We put them on one plain page per state or city, in plain English.

How we research

  • Every rule, deadline and fee comes from the statute text (from the state legislature or a reliable copy of the code) or an official agency page.
  • We link each fact to the page it came from and show the date we last checked it.
  • If we can't confirm something in the law or on an official page, we leave it out instead of guessing. We don't publish storage prices unless an official source states them.
  • We re-check pages regularly and when a state changes its law.

We are not lawyers, and nothing here is legal advice. We are an independent site, not affiliated with any storage company, government agency or court. Your rental agreement matters too, so read it, and talk to a lawyer or free legal aid about your situation.

How we make money

Some pages include a "Compare other storage options" box with links to storage marketplaces (SpareFoot, Storage.com and Neighbor). Those are paid partner links: if you rent through one, we may earn a commission, at no extra cost to you. Partners never pay to change what a guide says. Links to legal aid and pro bono services are never paid. Read our full disclosure and privacy policy.
