## How we research

- Every rule, deadline and fee comes from the **statute text** (from the state legislature or a reliable copy of the code) or an **official agency page**.
- We link each fact to the page it came from and show the date we last checked it.
- If we can't confirm something in the law or on an official page, we leave it out instead of guessing. We don't publish storage prices unless an official source states them.
- We re-check pages regularly and when a state changes its law.

We are not lawyers, and nothing here is legal advice. We are an independent site, not affiliated with any storage company, government agency or court. Your rental agreement matters too, so read it, and talk to a lawyer or [free legal aid](/free-legal-help/) about your situation.
